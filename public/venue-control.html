Venue Control

This will control the DirecTV receivers and TVs on the headend — changing channels, turning TVs on and off, and applying whole-room presets — from this iPad or from here remotely.

Not built yet. The design spec lives in docs/venue-control.md in the repo. First up is a discovery/diagnostics pass at the bar to map out exactly which TVs speak which control protocol — nothing here will do anything until that's done and an on-site control box is running.
