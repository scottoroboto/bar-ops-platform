Venue Control

This controls the DirecTV receivers and TVs on the headend — changing channels, turning TVs on and off, and applying whole-room presets — from this iPad or from here remotely.

Discovery & Diagnostics (Phase 1), DirecTV source control (Phase 2), and TV power with schedules (Phase 3) are built and live on the on-site agent box. TV source (channel) selection and whole-room layouts are still ahead — see docs/venue-control.md in the repo for the full plan. The cards below manage the receiver/TV inventory, zones, favorites, and schedules; day-to-day control happens on the agent's own Sources/TVs tabs at the bar, not here.

Sites

Turn Venue Control on or off per location. A location with Venue Control off just doesn't have a site set up yet — nothing else about it changes.

The receiver inventory that feeds the QAM plant — slot and QAM channel must be unique per location. Day-to-day channel changes happen on the agent's own Sources tab at the bar, not here.

Favorites

Zones

Physical TV groupings (e.g. "Patio", "Main Bar") used for zone-wide power on the agent's TVs tab and for zone-scoped schedules. Deleting a zone just un-assigns its TVs — nothing about the TVs themselves is lost.

TVs

The TV inventory that powers the agent's TVs tab. A TV needs a control method (found via Discovery, or set by hand) before it can actually be turned on/off from here.

Schedules

Fire automatically in this location's own timezone, evaluated on the agent box — e.g. all TVs on at 10:45, all off at 1:30 AM. Whole-room layout schedules aren't runnable yet (Phase 5); they can be created but won't do anything when they fire.
