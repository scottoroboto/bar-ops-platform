Venue Control

This controls the DirecTV receivers and TVs on the headend — changing channels, turning TVs on and off, and applying whole-room presets — from this iPad or from here remotely.

Discovery & Diagnostics (Phase 1), DirecTV source control (Phase 2), TV power with schedules (Phase 3), TV source (channel) selection (Phase 4), and whole-room layouts with backup/ restore and an activity log (Phase 5) are built and live. See docs/venue-control.md in the repo for the full plan. The cards below manage the receiver/TV inventory, zones, favorites, layouts, schedules, backups, and the activity log; day-to-day control happens on the agent's own Sources/TVs/Layouts tabs at the bar, not here. Capturing a layout's current-state items and taking/restoring a backup from the bar itself both happen on the agent's own Admin page too, so a new box can be brought back up without needing this page — see docs/venue-control.md §6.

Zones

Physical TV groupings (e.g. "Patio", "Main Bar") used for zone-wide power on the agent's TVs tab and for zone-scoped schedules. Deleting a zone just un-assigns its TVs — nothing about the TVs themselves is lost.

TVs

The TV inventory that powers the agent's TVs tab. A TV needs a control method (found via Discovery, or set by hand) before it can actually be turned on/off from here.

Schedules

Fire automatically in this location's own timezone, evaluated on the agent box — e.g. all TVs on at 10:45, all off at 1:30 AM.
