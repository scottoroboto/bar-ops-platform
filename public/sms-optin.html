How employees opt in to text alerts

Bar Ops, the staff app for Ticket Sports Bar

Bar Ops is a private employee app. It is behind a login, and accounts are created only by the owner for current employees. This page shows the exact opt-in form an employee sees inside the app (Systems Monitoring → My alert settings) and what they see after saving. Texts are off by default; nobody can opt in on another person's behalf.

1. The form, as shown in the app

Systems Monitoring → My alert settings

Texts go to the mobile number under My Account. Add it there first if it is missing.

The employee must choose "Text (SMS)" (or "Both") and tick the box themselves. Save is refused if the box is not ticked or the account has no mobile number.

2. What the employee sees after saving

Saved.

Agreed 10/13/2026 for +1 (850) 555-0100. Texts go to the mobile number under My Account.

The date, time and mobile number of consent are stored with the employee's account. Switching back to Email, replying STOP, or asking the owner ends texts.

3. The messages

Only operational alerts to employees who opted in. Examples:

  • Bar Ops: Walk-in cooler at Ticket 2 is 44°F, above the 40°F limit for 15 min. Open the app to acknowledge. Reply STOP to opt out.
  • Bar Ops: UDM Pro at Ticket 1 has been offline for 3 min. Reply STOP to opt out.
  • Bar Ops: the schedule for the week of Oct 13 is published. Open the app to view your shifts. Reply STOP to opt out.

STOP ends texts. HELP returns: "Bar Ops (Ticket Sports Bar) employee alerts. For help email info@ticketsportsbar.com. Reply STOP to unsubscribe." No marketing is ever sent.
